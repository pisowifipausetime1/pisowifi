Ever paid for an hour of Piso WiFi, stepped away for lunch, and come back to find your time nearly gone? It happens to almost everyone. Piso WiFi pause time is the feature that fixes this. It lets you freeze your remaining minutes and continue later, so you only pay for the internet you actually use.

What Is Piso WiFi Pause Time?

Piso WiFi is a coin-operated internet service found in sari-sari stores, dorms, terminals, and waiting areas across the Philippines. You insert a coin, or pay by GCash or Maya on newer machines, and receive a set amount of internet time.

Normally that timer keeps running whether you are online or not. Pause time stops it. When you tap the Pause button, your remaining balance is saved and nothing is deducted until you choose to resume.

How Does It Work?

The saving happens on the machine itself, not on your phone. When you pause, the Piso WiFi server stores your leftover time and links it to your device's MAC address, which is a unique ID for your phone or laptop. This is why closing your browser or locking your screen does not erase your balance.

It also explains why you must resume on the same device. A session paused on your phone cannot be continued on your laptop, because the machine only recognizes the original device.

How to Pause and Resume Your Session

  1. Connect to the WiFi. Choose the Piso WiFi network from your phone's settings. Most are open and need no password.
  2. Open the portal. Type http://10.0.0.1 directly into your browser's address bar. Do not search for it on Google.
  3. Start a session. Insert coins, enter a voucher code, or pay digitally, then wait for the timer to appear.
  4. Tap Pause Time. The button usually sits near the countdown. The timer freezes right away.
  5. Resume later. Reconnect on the same device, open the portal again, and tap Resume Time (some machines say "Continue"). Your countdown restarts from where it stopped.

Important Tips Before You Pause

  • Pause first, then leave. Turning off WiFi or walking out of range does not stop the timer on most machines. Only the Pause button does.
  • Use one device and one browser. Switching between them can cause problems with your saved balance.
  • Do not wait too long. Many owners set a maximum pause duration, and paused time can expire after a day or less.
  • Turn off your VPN. A VPN can stop the 10.0.0.1 page from loading.
  • On Android, disable mobile data and Private DNS if the portal will not open.

Why Is There No Pause Button?

Pause time is an optional setting, and the machine owner must switch it on. If you cannot see the button, it is most likely disabled. You cannot turn it on as a customer, but you can try these options:

  • Ask the shop owner to pause your session from the admin panel.
  • Buy a voucher and enter it only when you are ready to use it.
  • Top up in small amounts instead of buying a full hour.

If the button exists but does not work, clear your browser cache or open the portal in an incognito tab.

How Owners Can Enable Pause Time

Quick path for owners

If you run a Piso WiFi machine, enabling this feature is free and quick. Open http://10.0.0.1/admin, log in, go to Portal Settings, and turn on the Pause Time option. You can also set a maximum pause length. Save your changes and restart the machine if needed.

Menu names differ slightly between LPB Piso WiFi, AdoPiSoft, and PisoFi, so check your own panel.

Customers appreciate not losing their minutes, and happy customers tend to return. Also remember to change the default admin password to keep your machine secure. More setup help is in our Piso WiFi guides.

Is Pause Time Safe and Reliable?

The feature itself is safe, but remember that public Piso WiFi networks are usually not encrypted. Avoid banking apps and sensitive logins while connected. Also note that on some machines a power cut or reboot can erase a paused session, since the data is stored locally. A few systems, such as PisoFi, back up sessions to the cloud, which offers better protection.

Final Thoughts

Piso WiFi pause time is a simple feature that saves real money. Connect, pay, pause when you step away, and resume on the same device. Check for the Pause button before you pay, since not every machine has it enabled. If you are an owner, turning it on takes only a few minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does pausing use my paid balance?

No. Your remaining time stays untouched while the session is paused.

Can I resume on a different phone?

No. Sessions are tied to the device that started them.

What happens if I close my browser while paused?

Nothing. Your time is stored on the machine, so just reopen the portal to resume.

Can I leave a session paused for days?

It is not recommended. Many machines expire paused sessions after a set limit, often within 24 hours.

Why won't the portal open?

Check that you are on the right network, turn off any VPN, and type the address manually instead of searching for it. Our 10.0.0.1 guide has more fixes.
